## 0.1.0

### Feat

- browser artillery game with destructible terrain, ballistics, weapons shop, scoring and AI (easy to expert)
- low-poly 3D tank and shell models from a shared spec, with Blender build script and glTF exports
- online multiplayer: lobby, rooms with vote-to-start, chat, bots, reconnect/autopilot and spectators
- container image, production Compose and GitHub Actions publish pipeline
